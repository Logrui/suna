# /firmoverview

Review a venture capital firm and provide structured information about its investment portfolio and mission.

## Purpose
This command analyzes venture capital firms to extract and organize key information about their investment thesis, portfolio companies, and recent activity. Use this when researching VC firms, understanding their investment focus, or analyzing portfolio diversification.

## Instructions
- Review the aforementioned or linked venture capital firm
- Extract the firm name and create a concise mission summary
- Identify the investment categories/thesis areas the firm focuses on
- Organize portfolio companies by their categories when available (or generalize if not categorized)
- Research and include recent activity or news about the firm and its portfolio companies

## Output Format
Provide the information in the following structured format:

**Name:** (name of the firm)

**Mission Summary:** (brief overview of the firm's investment thesis and approach)

**Categories:** (investment focus areas, e.g., Vertical AI, Deep Tech, Health and Bio)

**Portfolio Companies:**
- [Category Name]: (comma-separated list of companies)
- [Category Name]: (comma-separated list of companies)

**Recent Activity/News:** (notable recent announcements, funding rounds, or portfolio company updates)

## Best Practices
- Be concise but informative in mission summaries
- Organize portfolio companies by the categories the firm explicitly uses when available
- Include company names that are verifiable and relevant to the firm's stated thesis
- Flag if information appears outdated and recommend verification
- Note any notable exits, acquisitions, or IPOs in the portfolio

## Example Output

**Name:** 2048 Ventures

**Mission Summary:** 2048 Ventures is a thesis-driven early stage venture capital firm based in NYC and Boston. They write $500K - $2M checks and lead Pre-Seed or Seed rounds in companies focused on vertical AI, deep tech, health, and bio sectors.

**Categories:** Vertical AI, Deep Tech, Health and Bio

**Portfolio Companies:**
- Vertical AI: Gloss Genius, Soona, Vitally
- Deep Tech: (company list)
- Health: (company list)
- Bio: (company list)

**Recent Activity/News:** Pageport Launches AI-Powered CRM Slant, Raises $3.3M Seed Round
